01

Overview

Time 2 Play Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) values your privacy. This policy describes general practices for our website and mobile games. Individual games may provide additional notices that explain their specific data practices.

02

Information we collect

The information collected depends on how you use our services and which game you play. It may include:

  • Information you provide: such as your name, email address, and the content of messages you send us.
  • Technical and usage information: such as device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate region, and interaction or diagnostic data.
  • Advertising and analytics data: some games may use third-party services that collect identifiers or events to measure performance, understand usage, prevent fraud, or provide advertising.

We do not intend to collect information that is unnecessary to operate, support, or improve our services.

03

How information is used

Information may be used to provide and maintain games, respond to support requests, diagnose technical issues, improve gameplay and app performance, understand aggregate usage, protect against abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

04

Ads & analytics

Our mobile games may use third-party advertising, mediation, crash reporting, or analytics services. These providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, app events, and related technical data under their own privacy policies. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have options to manage consent or personalized advertising through in-app settings or your device settings.

Before publishing, update this section to name the advertising and analytics SDKs actually used in each game and link to their current privacy disclosures.

05

Children’s privacy

Our services are not intended to collect personal information from children in a way that violates applicable law. Where a game is designed for children or may be used by children, we take the steps required by applicable laws and platform policies. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information can contact us so we can review the request.

Make sure this wording accurately reflects each game's target audience, age rating, and applicable child-directed service requirements before publishing.

06

Sharing & retention

We may share relevant information with service providers that help us operate, analyze, secure, or support our services, or when required by law. We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

07

Your choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can also manage device permissions and advertising preferences through available device or app settings. Contact us to submit a privacy request.

08

Policy changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date when changes are made. Any additional notice required by law will be provided where appropriate.

09

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@time2playgames.site. Replace this address if you use a different contact email.
